Offer Description
The competition is open to candidates who meet the requirements specified in Article 113 of the Act of 20 July 2018 – Law on Higher Education and Science (Dz. U. 2018, item 1668, as amended).
Key Responsibilities:
- Delivering a range of teaching and mentoring activities,
- Teaching architectural and urban design according to the annual hours requirement (incl. office hours, assessment, and examinations),
- Contributing to university organizational activities,
- Continuous professional development,
- Engaging in research and scientific-technical activities,
- Assisting in paid research and development activities for enterprises,
- Supporting student scientific activities and initiatives,
- Undertaking a range of tasks assigned by the direct supervisor.
Where to apply
- wbia@tu.kielce.pl
Requirements
- Research Field
- Architecture » Design
- Education Level
- Master Degree or equivalent
- Master’s degree in architecture (or equivalent professional degree),
- Aptitude for teaching and academic research,
- Documented teaching experience at the university level (minimum 4 years),
- Professional experience supported by relevant professional qualifications/licenses in architectural design or construction management (minimum 5 years),
- Skilled in industry-standard software, incl. AutoCAD, ArchiCAD, Photoshop, Revit,
- Good command of English,
- For foreign nationals: fluency in spoken and written Polish at a level allowing for independent teaching.
