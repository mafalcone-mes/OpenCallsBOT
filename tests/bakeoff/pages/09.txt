Offer Description
Industrial Design is a broad and evolving field that engages with environmental, societal and technological challenges through design practice, research and experimentation. We are looking for an innovative and curious professor who wants to inspire and educate the designers of tomorrow, and who can contribute to the continued development of the subject through design practice, research and collaborations.
Candidates from Sweden and other countries are invited to apply. All documents submitted for the application should be in English.
Vi välkomnar ansökningar från kandidater i Sverige och internationellt. Den svenska versionen av annonsen ligger här: Lediga anställningar - Konstfack
Our recruitments are based on expertise and carried out in accordance with the University’s aim to increase diversity and to be free from discrimination.
Konstfack is Sweden's largest university for arts, crafts and design. Since 1844, we have educated a diverse range of professionals within these fields as well as art and sloyd teachers. We conduct both artistic and scientific education/research and currently have approximately 900 students and 200 employees. Please read more at www.konstfack.se
The position is based in the Design specialisation at the Department of Design, Interior Architecture and Visual Communication (DIV), where both education and research are conducted in these fields. The department offers education at the Bachelor’s, Master’s, and Doctoral levels. At the department, artistic practice is integrated with critical and societally oriented knowledge development. DIV’s research environment is under development, and as a professor you are expected to share responsibility for leading its development within the Design specialisation.
The two programmes offered within the Design specialisation follows a progressive educational structure with a strong foundation in sustainability. On the Bachelor’s programme in Industrial Design (180 credits), students gain a broad foundation in industrial design through artistic creation, form-giving and design methodology, whilst exploring materials, techniques and digital media in relation to users. The students develop physical and digital skills, and gain understanding of the profession, through collaboration with industry and society. The Master’s Programme Design Ecologies (120 credits) builds on this foundation, expanding theoretical and methodological knowledge, maintaining a strong grounding in material and spatial practice, while acknowledging humans' interdependency with other species. This prepares students for both professional and research-oriented careers.
Main work duties
With this recruitment there will be two professors leading the Design specialisation. The Professors share artistic and pedagogical responsibility for the subject areas. A key responsibility of the Professors is to actively contribute to the development of the research environment within the subject area and at Konstfack in general.
The duties include:
- Shared responsibility for the artistic and educational leadership within the department as well as the development of the subject area
- Actively participating in teaching at Bachelor’s, Master’s, and Doctoral levels
- Strengthening national and international collaborations with industry, academia and societal stakeholders
- Conducting research within the subject area
- Leading, developing and expanding the research environment
- Initiating research applications and seeking external funding nationally and internationally
- Participating in Konstfack’s joint activities, as well as being part of relevant working groups
The position
The position comprises 60-100 percent of full-time employment for a maximum of five years, with the possibility of renewal for a further maximum of five years. The proposed start date of employment is between April-June 2027. Working hours are to be carried out at Konstfack with several sessions each working week. Remote work from abroad is not possible. In Sweden, there is a maintenance requirement for those applying for a work permit. To find out how this affects you as an international applicant, please read about the maintenance requirement for work permits on the Migration Agency's website or contact HR@konstfack.se.
Eligibility requirements and assessment criteria
The eligibility requirements and assessment criteria for the appointment of teaching staff can be found in the Higher Education Ordinance and in the Konstfack Appointments Procedure. Eligibility requirements are the minimum requirements that need to be met in order for a candidate to be eligible for employment. The assessment criteria are those factors that are assessed in addition to the eligibility requirements, if the eligibility requirements are met.
The eligibility requirements for this position are:
- A university degree at advanced level or higher, relevant to the role, or other professional experience assessed equivalent in relation to the position
- Documented pedagogical proficiency in higher education or in another relevant educational context
- Documented professional practice in industrial design and/or product design
- Documented experience of collaboration within or outside academiaExperience of conducting research relevant to the subject area
- Artistic expertise
- Good command of English
The assessment criteria for this position are:
- Particular expertise in industrial design and/or product design, demonstrated through established professional and/or research experience.
- Solid experience of leading collaborative development work, as well as strong communication skills
- Strong ability to initiate, lead and develop research projects and research environments, as well as to attract external funding
- Good pedagogical proficiency
- Good artistic expertise
- Good ability to integrate critical perspectives, including those relating to sustainability and transition, into research and teaching
- Good knowledge of current research in the field of industrial design and/or product design
- Doctoral degree within a relevant subject area
- Active in national and/or international networks relevant to the subject area
- Good organisational and administrative skills
- Good language skills in Swedish or another Scandinavian language
Training in higher education teaching and learning in higher education
For employment as a professor, senior lecturer or lecturer (with a period of employment longer than two years), the employee must have completed training in higher education teaching and learning in higher education at Konstfack or another university or have acquired corresponding knowledge in another way. An applicant who is otherwise deemed eligible but has not completed training in teaching and learning in higher education is to start the training (equivalent to 7.5 credits) in order to acquire the missing qualification within one year from the start of the appointment.
