# Oppo · beta

Oppo is a little robot that looks for jobs, residencies, open calls and grants
that fit your work. It runs on your computer: your portfolio and what you tell
it stay with you.

**This is a test copy. It works for 5 days from the first time you open it.**
At the end, tell us how it went: [feedback form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfdmveaSkqL7pwKzj4ByDDUQUXN6G4stlJMF6OcjtAPv4iA5Q/viewform).

*(Italiano sotto.)*

## Install

You need a computer with at least 8 GB of memory (16 GB is better) and about
15 GB of free space.

1. **Install Ollama** (free, it runs the AI models): [ollama.com/download](https://ollama.com/download).
   Open it once.
2. **Download Oppo** from [Releases](../../releases/latest): the `windows` zip or the `macos` zip.
3. **Unzip it** and open it:
   - **Windows**: double-click `Oppo.exe`. If Windows says it protected your PC:
     *More info* → *Run anyway*.
   - **Mac**: right-click `Open Oppo.command` → *Open* → *Open* (only the first time).
4. Your browser opens on the **Welcome** page: add your portfolio PDF, say what
   you are looking for, and Oppo downloads its models (a few GB, once).
5. Press **Search my sources**. The first search takes about an hour.

A small window stays open while Oppo runs: close it to quit.

## Installazione (italiano)

Serve un computer con almeno 8 GB di memoria (meglio 16) e circa 15 GB liberi.

1. **Installa Ollama** (gratis, fa girare i modelli AI): [ollama.com/download](https://ollama.com/download). Aprilo una volta.
2. **Scarica Oppo** da [Releases](../../releases/latest): lo zip `windows` o `macos`.
3. **Estrai lo zip** e aprilo:
   - **Windows**: doppio clic su `Oppo.exe`. Se Windows dice che ha protetto il PC: *Ulteriori informazioni* → *Esegui comunque*.
   - **Mac**: clic destro su `Open Oppo.command` → *Apri* → *Apri* (solo la prima volta).
4. Il browser si apre sulla pagina **Welcome**: carica il PDF del tuo portfolio, scrivi cosa cerchi e Oppo scarica i suoi modelli (qualche GB, una volta sola).
5. Premi **Search my sources**. La prima ricerca dura circa un'ora.

Una piccola finestra resta aperta mentre Oppo funziona: chiudila per uscire.

**Questa è una copia di prova: funziona per 5 giorni dalla prima apertura.**
